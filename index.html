House Rules

📢

General Conduct

  • Keep phone conversations private; use the terrace for loud calls.
  • Follow the "Leave it as you found it" policy for cleanliness.
  • Resolve conflicts via the PG owner; do not take matters into your own hands.
  • Turn off all lights when leaving to conserve energy.
🌙

Night & Curfew

  • Arrival deadline: 10:00 PM. Notify owner for rare delays.
  • Lights out by 10:30 PM in shared rooms.
  • After 10:30 PM: Use the 4th floor (CMG) or terrace/corridor for work.
  • Inform the owner in advance if you will be away for the night.
  • Do not use Washing machine after 10PM
  • Curfew is strictly enforced; violations may lead to eviction.
🚫

Visitor Policy

  • Strictly no outside friends allowed inside the premises.
  • No delivery personnel (Swiggy/Zomato/Courier) beyond the gate.
  • Parents cannot stay overnight or enter without prior permission.
🔑

Departure & Fees

  • Return locker keys, pillows, and bedding in original condition.
  • Overstaying the contract results in a fee of ₹600 per day.
🔑

Absence Policy

  • If you are unable to return for a night to the PG, inform the owner in advance.
  • If you are away due to travel, inform the owner.
  • Failure to inform the owner may result in penalties and/or eviction from PG
🔑

Hygiene

  • Keep your room and common areas clean and tidy.
  • Dispose of waste in the designated bins.
  • Report any hygiene issues to the management immediately.
  • You are responsible to clean the rubbish created by you.
  • Fridge will not be cleaned. You need to clean it and keep it free old/spoilt items.
